🏦 Migrating Millions of Banking Customer Entitlements with Zero Downtime

Macquarie Group - Enterprise Banking Transformation

The Challenge

Macquarie Group's customer entitlements — governing who can access which banking accounts and in what capacity on every internet banking request — were spread across legacy platforms including Siebel CRM, a legacy Oracle Coherence cache, and legacy Business Banking entitlement systems. Serving tens of millions of daily requests, the bank needed to migrate all Business Banking, Personal Banking, and Private Banking products to a strategic cloud-native store without disrupting a single customer transaction, while preparing the platform to scale toward hundreds of millions of daily requests.

My Solution

  • Cross-Platform Architecture & Strangler Fig Migration: As Initiative and Platform Tech Lead across 8–10 enterprise platforms, authored Level-1 cross-platform and Level-2 detailed designs to migrate entitlements product by product from legacy relational/Siebel stores to the strategic DSE Graph platform on GCP (Spring Boot, GKE, Apigee X, Pub/Sub, Cloud Spanner, BigQuery, Dataflow).
  • Dark Launch & Automated Reconciliation: Executed every migration — across Business Banking, Home Loans, Offsets, white-label Transaction & Savings, Term Deposits (~5 months), and Wealth (~4 months) — using dark launches with zero downtime and fully automated reconciliation against legacy sources.
  • Next-Generation Scale & Performance: Planned DSE Graph capacity growth from 6 to 18 nodes over five years, introduced use-case APIs with Protobuf alongside JSON for Open Payments Platform, and load-tested a next-gen POC with synthetic data for 40M customers at 5,000 TPS (vs ~600 avg / ~1,000 peak today) using Redis caching to meet a 100 ms p99 SLA.
  • AI-Assisted Delivery & Team Growth: Championed GitHub Copilot and Claude Code (extended with MCP servers and custom design Skills) on the Term Deposits and Wealth migrations, while helping grow the team from 6 to 20+ engineers and mentoring engineers into area leads.

Measurable Results

  • ✅ 100% uptime across all product-by-product migrations of millions of entitlement records
  • 💰 Tens of millions saved annually in licence, support, and infrastructure costs by enabling Siebel CRM and legacy Oracle Coherence cache decommission
  • 🤖 ~3x faster delivery and lower cost on Term Deposits & Wealth migrations versus comparable earlier migrations via AI-assisted development
  • ⚡ Validated 5,000 TPS at 100 ms p99 SLA for the next-generation entitlements platform scale-up toward hundreds of millions of daily requests
  • 👥 Scaled team from 6 to 20+ engineers, mentoring engineers into permanent area-lead roles

Key Insight

The secret wasn't just technical prowess — it was understanding that in banking, trust is everything. Every decision prioritised customer confidence over speed.

🛡️ Building a Cloud-Native Insurance Platform from Scratch

Aegon Life - Startup-Speed Enterprise Solution

The Challenge

Aegon Life Insurance (now Bandhan Life Insurance) needed to build a headless, cloud-native, multi-tenant life insurance administration system on AWS from concept to production in a startup environment – fast iteration with enterprise reliability. The platform needed multi-tenant B2B APIs for major e-commerce partners alongside direct-to-consumer digital portals while maintaining strict compliance standards.

My Solution

  • End-to-End Cloud-Native Architecture: As founding Principal Engineer partnering with the CTO and VP Engineering, owned architecture, coding, DevOps, and production across the full policy lifecycle on AWS (Spring Boot, Lambda, EKS, API Gateway, Camunda, Step Functions, SQS/SNS, RDS, DynamoDB, Neptune, ElastiCache, Micro frontends & self-serve PWA backed by a BFF layer).
  • Reusable Microservice Foundations: Built shared foundations for logging, tracing, distributed caching, messaging, Lambda/BPMN orchestration, PDF document generation, document management, and notifications.
  • Multi-Tenant API-First Platform: Designed multi-tenant B2B APIs enabling straight-through Group policy issuance and rapid onboarding of major e-commerce partners, plus a conversational-AI POC (Amazon Lex, BERT, AWS Neptune, GraphQL).
  • Governance & Scaling Engineering: Ran architecture review forums and cross-squad standards while helping scale engineering from 10 to 40 across Aegon Life, Saksoft, and Thoughtworks.

Measurable Results

  • 🚀 Launched first Group insurance e-commerce partnership in 6 weeks (insureMO case study) and onboarded 5 B2B partners
  • 🚀 Enabled Pure Digital 2.0 strategy, shutting down physical touch points across India (read Pure Digital 2.0 interview)
  • ⚡ Faster time-to-market for new insurance products — first insurer in India to launch Covid-19 coverage during the pandemic (Covid-19 coverage news article)
  • 👥 Scaled engineering from 10 to 40 with reusable platform building blocks adopted across squads
  • 🏆 Red Carpet Award and Star of the Quarter recognition (CTO recognition post)

Key Insight

Startup speed doesn't mean sacrificing enterprise quality. The right architecture enables both agility and reliability.

🤖 Solving AI Coding Costs with Hybrid Multi-Agent Orchestration (MACA)

Open-Source R&D - Local-First Hybrid AI Coding Assistant

The Challenge

Pure cloud-first AI coding workflows become expensive quickly when every routine task is sent to a frontier cloud model, while single-shot prompt wrappers frequently produce incomplete changes or leave half-finished code scattered directly in the developer's working tree.

My Solution

  • Tiered Local + Cloud Routing: Built a complexity router that keeps simple tasks free and on-device via local Gemma (Ollama), escalates medium tasks to Google Gemini, and reserves Anthropic Claude for complex multi-file reasoning (with automatic fallbacks).
  • Spec-Driven Multi-Agent Pipeline: Coordinated specialized agents for task classification, greenfield stack clarification, implementation planning, and human-in-the-loop technical specifications before coding begins.
  • Isolated Staging & Verification Loops: Implemented incremental disk staging with automated code-completion verification and line-diff review loops, promoting files to the workspace only after review approval.

Highlights & Impact

  • 💻 Zero token billing on routine tasks by running simple work on local compute (practical on an Apple M3 Pro 18GB laptop and heavier models on a standalone Apple M1 Max over Ollama)
  • 🛡️ Clean workspace boundary — generated code is staged, verified against the plan/spec, and audited before touching repository files
  • 🔓 Open-source alpha on GitHub: GSSwain/multi-agent-coding-assistant · Read the MACA architecture post

Key Insight

The best AI-assisted coding workflow isn't using the biggest model for everything – it's routing each task to the right model tier with spec-driven guardrails around it.
